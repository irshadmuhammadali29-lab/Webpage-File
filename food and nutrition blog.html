Extra info

Hydration & Nutrition: Aim for 2 to 3 liters of water daily and a diet rich in whole foods, vegetables, and lean proteins.


Daily Movement: Incorporate at least 30 minutes of moderate activity, such as brisk walking, cycling, or swimming.


Quality Sleep: Allow 7 to 9 hours of rest by maintaining a consistent sleep schedule and limiting screen time an hour before bed.


Mindful Breaks: Take short moments throughout the day to decompress, stretch, or practice gratitude to lower stress levels.
